A pointer drawn on the icon grid

Twenty-five drawings on the same 24-unit grid as the icons, in the two themes Bioma builds from the palette, derived the way core/Theme.qml derives it. Hover any drawing and your real pointer becomes it. The specification is CURSORS.md; the drawings are a copy of assets/cursors/drawings.json.

Decided

  • Themes

    Bioma is the default: background body, text contour. It reads on every backdrop, and the palette comes in only through the node.

    Bioma Cell ships beside it, the more faithful to the cells. A primary-filled variant was dropped: it made the cursor the brightest thing on screen, all day.

  • The node

    The primary dot inside the arrow is the thread's node, the point where the pointer attaches to the world.

  • Shapes

    The arrow is soft, after Bibata Modern Classic: no tail, every corner a true arc. The hands are outlines only, their fingers overlapping so no seam shows.

  • Wait

    Wait and progress carry the loader, under the exception LOADER.md already makes: a busy cursor means "no value yet". With the animations switched off, the segment holds still at the left end.

  • Not allowed

    No red. A refused drop is not a measurement past threshold.
